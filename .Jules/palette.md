## 2026-07-16 - Support Menu Polishing & Tap-to-Copy
**Learning:** Monospace formatting enables 'tap-to-copy' on Telegram, which is extremely useful for copying support phone numbers. At the same time, @ handles should NOT be formatted with backticks as it disables their clickability.
**Action:** Use monospace for phone numbers and keys, keep Telegram handles unformatted so they can be clicked.
