## 2026-06-16 - [Monospace Tap-to-Copy]
**Learning:** In Telegram, text wrapped in monospace backticks (e.g., `text`) is rendered in a fixed-width font and enables a "tap-to-copy" feature on mobile clients, which significantly reduces friction for users needing to copy balances, account numbers, or phone numbers.
**Action:** Use monospace formatting for all critical financial and contact details in Telegram bots to improve usability.
