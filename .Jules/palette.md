## 2026-07-16 - Payment Instructions Tap-to-Copy UX
**Learning:** Wrapping critical financial details (like CBE account numbers, Telebirr phone numbers, and cleaned amounts) in monospace backticks (`...`) in Telegram Markdown enables single-tap copying. This drastically reduces manual typing friction and errors during payment workflows.
**Action:** Always wrap account numbers, phone numbers, transaction IDs, and clean amounts in monospace backtick formatting when displaying payment instructions or financial information in Telegram bots.
