## 2024-06-05 - Tap-to-Copy and Onboarding Skip
**Learning:** Monospace formatting (backticks) in Telegram enables 'tap-to-copy', which is a critical accessibility and UX feature for financial details like account numbers and phone numbers. Skipping onboarding for returning users reduces friction.
**Action:** Always use monospace for IDs, account numbers, and phone numbers. Check user registration status in the `/start` command.
