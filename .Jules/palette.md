## 2026-07-16 - Support Menu Polishing
**Learning:** Monospace formatting allows tap-to-copy in Telegram clients, which is extremely useful for telephone numbers and financial details. However, wrapping Telegram handles (e.g., `@username`) in monospace blocks prevents them from being parsed as clickable links.
**Action:** Always present telephone numbers or account numbers in monospace blocks (using backticks) to facilitate tap-to-copy, but keep Telegram handles unformatted or as standard links so they remain directly clickable.
