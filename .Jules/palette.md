## 2026-07-16 - Payment Instruction Tap-to-Copy UX Polish
**Learning:** Monospace formatting (backticks) on critical payment inputs (account numbers, phone numbers, and amounts) enables instant tap-to-copy functionality in Telegram clients, reducing friction and potential typos during financial transactions.
**Action:** Always format sensitive identifiers like bank account numbers and payment amounts in code blocks using `parse_mode="Markdown"` when presenting transaction details to the user.
