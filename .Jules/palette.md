## 2026-07-16 - Polishing Support Menu UX
**Learning:** In text-heavy chat platforms like Telegram, presenting contact information cleanly with structured lists, bold headers, and monospace blocks significantly improves clarity. Specifying phone numbers inside monospace backticks enables the convenient 'tap-to-copy' feature in most Telegram clients. Meanwhile, Telegram handles (e.g. `@username`) should not be wrapped in backticks to ensure they remain clickable links for instant communication.
**Action:** Always format support phone numbers inside monospace backticks (`+251712759695`) for tap-to-copy, keep Telegram handles as unformatted plaintext (or clickable link format) for native link-triggering behavior, and organize multiple lines of info using clean bulleted lists under bold headers.
