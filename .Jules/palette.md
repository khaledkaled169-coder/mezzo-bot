## 2026-06-10 - [Tap-to-Copy for Critical Identifiers]
**Learning:** In Telegram bots, monospace formatting (`backticks`) is essential for data that users need to input elsewhere (like bank account numbers or phone numbers), as it enables a "tap-to-copy" action that reduces friction and prevents errors.
**Action:** Always identify unique identifiers or copyable data and wrap them in monospace formatting with appropriate Markdown parse mode.
