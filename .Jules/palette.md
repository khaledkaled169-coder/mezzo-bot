## 2026-07-16 - Support Channel Copy Accessibility
**Learning:** Monospace formatting (backticks) in Telegram allows quick tap-to-copy, which is crucial for phone numbers. However, wrapping active handles or URLs in backticks disables their hyperlink functionality in several Telegram clients, reducing navigability.
**Action:** Always present telephone numbers or numeric account numbers inside monospace blocks (backticks) to enable tap-to-copy, while leaving handles (like @mezzodeposite) and links unformatted or wrapped in standard markdown link tags to preserve clickability.
