## 2026-07-16 - Support Contact UX Polish
**Learning:** Monospace backticks enabling tap-to-copy in Telegram clients must be reserved for alphanumeric values (like phone numbers) that are commonly copied, while Telegram handles (like `@mezzodeposite`) should remain unformatted to ensure they remain clickable links in most Telegram clients.
**Action:** When implementing Telegram bot contact or support menus, format phone numbers with monospace backticks and keep usernames or channels unformatted for optimal UX.
