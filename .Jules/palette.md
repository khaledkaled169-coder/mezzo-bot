## 2026-06-16 - Tap-to-Copy UX Pattern
**Learning:** In Telegram, monospace text (wrapped in backticks) is automatically copyable to the clipboard upon tapping. This is a massive UX win for account numbers, phone numbers, and transaction IDs, as it removes the friction of manual selection or switching between apps to transcribe.
**Action:** Always use monospace formatting for numeric or alphanumeric strings that users are likely to need to copy (e.g., bank accounts, payment amounts, handles).
