## 2026-06-16 - Tap-to-Copy Monospace Formatting
**Learning:** Telegram users on mobile benefit significantly from monospace formatting on critical data like account numbers and transaction IDs, as it enables "tap-to-copy" functionality, reducing manual entry errors.
**Action:** Always wrap account numbers, phone numbers, and financial IDs in backticks (monospace) and ensure dynamic data within those blocks is sanitized to prevent breaking the Markdown formatting.
