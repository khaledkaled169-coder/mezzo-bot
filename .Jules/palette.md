## 2026-06-06 - [Tap-to-copy formatting for critical data]
**Learning:** In Telegram bots, wrapping critical information like phone numbers, account numbers, and transaction IDs in monospace formatting (using backticks) provides an essential 'tap-to-copy' functionality. This significantly improves accessibility and reduces user friction during payment and verification processes.
**Action:** Always use monospace formatting for any financial details or identification numbers that users might need to copy and paste.
