## 2026-06-16 - Tap-to-Copy for Bot Micro-UX
**Learning:** Monospace formatting in Telegram (using backticks) is a powerful micro-UX pattern for financial and account details, as it enables "tap-to-copy" functionality on mobile devices.
**Action:** Always wrap account numbers, phone numbers, and transaction IDs in monospace backticks to improve user efficiency during manual payment or verification steps. Ensure dynamic data is cleaned of internal backticks and that Markdown special characters are escaped in the surrounding text to prevent parsing errors.
