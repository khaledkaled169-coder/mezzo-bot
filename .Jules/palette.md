## 2026-07-16 - Tap-to-Copy Phone Numbers in Support Menu
**Learning:** Using monospace formatting (backticks) for critical digits like support phone numbers allows users on mobile to easily 'tap-to-copy' them directly to their dialer or clipboard, drastically reducing transaction friction.
**Action:** Always apply monospace backtick formatting on phone numbers and other precise numerical fields in Telegram menus while keeping standard links like handles unformatted to preserve clickability.
