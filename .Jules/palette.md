## 2026-06-16 - [Tap-to-Copy for Financial Details]
**Learning:** Monospace formatting in Telegram is more than just visual—it provides a functional 'tap-to-copy' feature. This is a massive UX win for account numbers and transaction IDs, especially in payment flows where manual entry is prone to errors.
**Action:** Wrap critical numeric and alphanumeric identifiers in backticks (monospace) to enable easy copying for users.
