## 2026-07-16 - Safe Monospace Formatting and Mobile Tap-to-Copy
**Learning:** Wrapping critical financial details, transaction IDs, account numbers, and phone numbers in monospace backticks (Markdown V1) enables effortless, friction-free "tap-to-copy" on Telegram mobile clients. However, Telegram handles (such as `@mezzodeposite`) must never be formatted as monospace, as this prevents them from being parsed as clickable links. Additionally, dynamic content placed inside backticks must be sanitized using a robust helper (like `clean_mono`) to prevent unescaped internal backticks from breaking the Telegram message format, while ensuring zero-value balances are explicitly preserved and not ignored.
**Action:** For all future Telegram bot interfaces, design text outputs with clear section headers, utilize monospace formatting exclusively on high-friction copy targets (accounts, amounts, keys), avoid monospace formatting on interactive handles/links, and consistently apply sanitizers (like `clean_mono`) to dynamic content inside backticks.
