## 2026-06-16 - Tap-to-Copy for Account Details
**Learning:** Monospace formatting in Telegram messages enables the "tap-to-copy" feature, which significantly reduces friction for users when they need to copy account numbers, phone numbers, or transaction IDs for payments and support.
**Action:** Always use monospace formatting (backticks) for sensitive or actionable data like bank account numbers and phone numbers, and use bold headers for better visual hierarchy in bot messages.
